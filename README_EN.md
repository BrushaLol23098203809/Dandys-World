## Dandy's World 3D Simulator
This is Dandy's World 3D Simulator. A 3D Adventure.
## How to Play Dandy's World Simulator?
The Objective: You and your team must navigate the sprawling floors of the Gardenview museum and educational center. You extract "Eor" by completing tasks in timing-based mini-games and fill extractors to progress to the next floor.

Playable Toons: There are over 30 unique Toons to select and unlock. Each has different stats, abilities, and playstyles.

Trinkets & Items: You can equip Trinkets to customize your character's perks and buy useful items (like speed boosts) from Dandy's shop to survive.
## Game Lore

Beneath its colorful exterior, the game has a deep and dark story. The Gardenview Center was an educational center and the filming location for a popular children's TV show that ran from 1990 to 2002. The friendly characters, or "Toons," were originally brought to life using a black ink-like substance called Ichor. In 2002, the center was shut down due to a tragic "malfunction incident". Today, the deserted halls are haunted by the "Twisted"—the corrupted and monstrous forms of the original Toons.
